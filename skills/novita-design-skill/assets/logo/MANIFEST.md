## Usage

Prefer these bundled SVGs before fetching remote logo assets. Preserve each SVG's original aspect ratio and viewBox. Do not recolor, stretch, outline, shadow, or reconstruct the paths.

For web:

```html
<img src="/path/to/novita-logo.svg" alt="Novita AI" width="88" height="24" />
```

For PPT or static design, place the SVG directly on the canvas and keep it visually quiet.
